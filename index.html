Rakat Counter

Hands-free rak'ah counter for iPhone, Apple Watch, Android and Wear OS.

Choose the prayer, put your phone in your pocket or keep your watch on your wrist, and pray. Rakat Counter counts each rak'ah from the motion sensors and vibrates gently when your last rak'ah begins.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Motion data never leaves your device.
