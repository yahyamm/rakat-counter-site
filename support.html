Support

Questions, problems or ideas: open an issue, or use the developer contact on the App Store or Google Play listing.

Getting the best count

  • Calibrate first. The 90-second calibration teaches the app how you stand, bow, prostrate and sit. Redo it if counts are off.
  • Phone: a chest (shirt) pocket is the most accurate. The trouser pocket is in beta.
  • Watch: wear it snugly. Counting continues with your wrist lowered.
  • Correct a count: tap + / − on the phone, or turn the Digital Crown / rotary input on the watch.

Prayer times look wrong

Open Settings → Prayer times and pick the calculation method and Asr method your mosque uses. You can add minute adjustments for each prayer.

Android: counting stops with the screen off

Some phones stop background apps to save battery. Allow notifications for Rakat Counter and turn off battery optimisation for it in system settings.

Rakat Counter is an aid. If you are unsure about your count, follow your own certainty.
