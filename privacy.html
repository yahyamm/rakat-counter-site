Rakat Counter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Rakat Counter is built to keep your data on your own devices.

What the app uses

DataWhyWhere it goes
Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, barometer)To recognize prayer positions and count rak'ahsProcessed on the device in real time. Never stored as raw data and never sent anywhere.
Location (approximate)To calculate prayer times for where you areYour coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, are sent to the prayer-times service (UmmahAPI) to get the timetable. Nothing else is sent with them.
Profiles, settings, calibrations, prayer historySo the app works the way you set it upStored on your device. If you use a paired watch, it is copied between your phone and watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity or Google's Wear OS Data Layer.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.
  • Motion data is never uploaded.

Your choices

  • You can enter a city instead of granting location access.
  • On Android, the notification permission only lets the app show that a prayer is being counted.
  • Deleting a profile deletes its settings, calibration and history.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data from that device.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/yahyamm/rakat-counter-site/issues or contact the developer through the App Store / Google Play listing.
